Privacy Policy for Plonzo

Effective date: 4 October 2026
App: Plonzo — Cook What You Have (Android)
Developer: Vunaro Studios, operated by Chaluvadi Venkat, India
Contact: contact@vunarostudios.com

Summary

  • Plonzo has no accounts and no login, and we run no servers that receive your data.
  • The things you save in the app (your ingredients, favourites, shopping list, meal plan and so on) are stored on your phone only.
  • Plonzo shows a banner advertisement through Google AdMob. To show ads, Google collects some technical information from your device. Details are in the "Ads and Google AdMob" section below.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Plonzo shows Google's consent form and requests ads only once Google's consent process allows it.
  • Recipes, cooking steps and your saved items work without an internet connection. Ads, the ad consent form and this policy link need one.

Information stored on your device

Plonzo saves the following only on your own device, so the app can work:

  • The ingredients you have selected in "My Kitchen"
  • Your favourite recipes
  • Recipes you have marked as cooked, and the star ratings you gave them
  • Recipes you have recently viewed
  • Your shopping list and which items you have ticked off
  • Your meal plan, and the reminder times you set for each meal
  • Whether Healthy Mode is turned on
  • Your theme choice (System, Light or Dark)
  • Whether you have already seen the welcome screens

We do not receive this information. It is not uploaded by Plonzo and it is not shared with anyone by us.

If your phone has Android's backup feature turned on, Android (not Plonzo) may include this saved app data in your Google account backup, under Google's own privacy terms. You control this in your phone's backup settings.

Information we collect

None. Plonzo does not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, address, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app.

Ads and Google AdMob

Plonzo shows a banner advertisement, served by Google AdMob (a service of Google LLC). When an ad is requested, Google's ad software on your phone sends information to Google. According to Google, this can include:

  • Your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier)
  • Your IP address, from which Google can work out your approximate location (such as your city or region)
  • Your interactions with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped)
  • Technical and diagnostic information about your device and the app (such as device model, operating system version and error information)

Google uses this to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud, and to keep its services working. We do not receive this information. We only see summary reports from AdMob, such as how many times ads were shown and how much they earned. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): In these regions, Plonzo shows Google's consent form when you open the app, and requests ads only once Google's consent process says that is allowed. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. That entry appears only where Google requires it.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's Android settings (typically under Settings → Google → Ads; the exact path varies by phone).
  • Other regions: Where the consent form is not required, ads are shown without it, and the Android advertising ID controls above still apply.

Permissions

Plonzo asks for, or includes, the following Android permissions:

  • Notifications — to show your meal-plan reminders. Plonzo asks for this permission when you open the Meal Plan. If you decline, the app still works, but without reminders.
  • Run at startup — so that meal reminders you have set are restored after your phone restarts or the app is updated.
  • Keep screen awake — so the screen stays on while you follow a recipe in Cook Mode.
  • Vibrate — used by the reminder notifications.
  • Internet and network state — used for ads and for the ad consent form. Plonzo does not use the internet for any other purpose.
  • Advertising ID and related ad permissions — included by Google's ad software so that ads can be served and measured, as described above.
  • Background work — included by a library used by the ad software. Plonzo does not run its own background services.

Plonzo does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Sharing from the app

When you tap Share, Plonzo hands the recipe text to Android's share sheet. You choose which app receives it. Plonzo does not see or control what happens after that.

Links to this policy

Opening the Privacy Policy from Settings opens this page in your web browser. This website is operated by Vunaro Studios.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data on your device: You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → Plonzo → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app. Items you save can also be removed inside the app.
  • Data held by Google: Data that Google collects for ads is controlled by Google. See the "Your choices" section above and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Requests to us: We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or request anyway, email us and we will reply.

Children's privacy

Plonzo is a general-audience cooking app. It is not designed for or directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We will publish any changes at this same address and update the effective date above. Before we release any version of Plonzo that adds accounts, online features or a database, we will publish an updated policy that covers them first.
