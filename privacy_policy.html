Privacy Policy for Plonzo

Effective date: 5 July 2026
App: Plonzo — Cook What You Have
Developer: Venkat
Contact: plonzo.app@gmail.com

Summary

Plonzo is a 100% offline cooking app. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no login, no ads, no analytics, and no internet connection in normal use.

Information we collect

None. Plonzo does not collect any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or any other identifying information.

Information stored on your device

Plonzo saves a small amount of data only on your own device, to make the app work:

  • The ingredients you select in your kitchen
  • Your favourite recipes
  • Which recipes you have marked as cooked
  • Recipes you have recently viewed
  • Your shopping list

This information never leaves your phone. It is not uploaded, backed up to our servers (we have no servers), or shared with anyone. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted with it.

Permissions

Plonzo requests only one permission:

  • Keep screen awake (WAKE_LOCK): used to stop your screen from turning off while you are following a recipe in Cook Mode, so you don't have to touch the phone with messy hands.

Plonzo does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions in the released app.

Internet and third parties

The released version of Plonzo has no internet permission and makes no network connections. We do not use any third-party services, advertising networks, analytics tools, or trackers.

Children's privacy

Plonzo is safe for all ages. Because we collect no data at all, we collect no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a future version, the updated policy will be published at this same address and the effective date above will be updated.
